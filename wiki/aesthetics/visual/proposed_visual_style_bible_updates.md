## Fields to populate

### core visual identity
- **Current:** _(empty)_
- **Proposed:** Signal transmission aesthetic. The image is always a mediated broadcast — never a clean photograph, never a direct illustration. Every output carries the trace of its reproduction: halftone dots, scan lines, colour channel separation, grid multiplication. The core visual identity is the face-as-data: human features treated as signal to be transmitted, corrupted, multiplied, and received through noise. Hot magenta on black void is the default state — a screen emitting into darkness. No logos. No clean branding. The visual identity IS the processing pipeline: source photograph → ComfyUI img2img → halftone overlay → colour tint → grid composition → output. Abstract/typographic mark as avatar — glyph, symbol, or type treatment. No face in the avatar; the face is reserved for the processed grid works.

### recurring motifs
- **Current:** _(empty)_
- **Proposed:**
  - **Face grid**: 4x5 or 5x6 matrix of the same face under different colour treatments (magenta, green, red, amber, grey, desaturated). References CCTV contact sheets, facial recognition training sets, Warhol seriality. The face is a dataset, not a portrait.
  - **Halftone dot overlay**: visible dot-matrix pattern on all processed imagery. Creates newspaper/risograph/screen-print materiality. The image is always already printed, always already mediated.
  - **Urban palimpsest**: layered graffiti walls as texture substrate. Tags over tags, torn posters over wheat paste, mixed scripts (English, Japanese, Cyrillic, French, Arabic). The wall records time as accumulation.
  - **Dark silhouette against damaged texture**: figure absorbed into crosshatch/noise field, identity dissolving into ground. The no-face principle.
  - **Torn poster / décollage**: commercial or portrait imagery partially revealed through layers of peeling paper. Villeglé tradition instrumentalised.
  - **Aperture framing**: faces seen through rusted metal windows, chain-link, industrial cut-outs. The frame is always a constraint — you see through damage, not through design.
  - **Corrugated iron / industrial ruin**: Nauru phosphate infrastructure, East London shutters, construction barriers as visual environment.
- **Sources:** Full ART22 corpus; face grids in ComfyUI 00054–00063; walls in IMG_3075–3157, IMG_6611–6657; silhouette in ComfyUI_00038_; aperture in DSC_0700-2 3.jpg
- **Status:** pending approval

### colour palette tendencies
- **Current:** _(empty)_
- **Proposed:**
  - **Primary: Hot magenta / signal pink** (#FF00FF → #FF1493) — the transmission colour. Used as dominant tint over halftone face grids. Not decorative. Functions as broadcast frequency.
  - **Primary: Deep black** — active ground, not absence. Carries halftone dots, crosshatch texture, CRT noise. The void from which signals emerge.
  - **Secondary: Blood red / vermillion** (#CC0000 → #FF0000) — violence register. Merged faces, flesh-material, Yokota broken-mirrors palette. Used sparingly for maximum disruption.
  - **Secondary: Teal / cyan** (#008080 → #00CED1) — appears in face grid variations, East London signage, neon graffiti. Cool counterpoint to magenta heat.
  - **Accent: Ochre / gold** (#CC9900 → #DAA520) — Hans Arp torso warmth, documentary skin tones, wheat paste ageing. The human warmth register.
  - **Accent: Grey-green-rust** — Nauru industrial palette, corrugated iron oxidation, concrete weathering. The Pacific ruins register.
  - **Forbidden:** pastels, gradients, clean whites, sky blue, any colour that reads as "wellness" or "calm" or "corporate"
- **Sources:** Colour extracted across full ComfyUI series, documentary photographs, Nauru reference (1542590288735-nauru_nightmare 2.jpg), broken mirrors series

### composition rules
- **Current:** _(empty)_
- **Proposed:**
  - **Grid-first**: default composition for processed face imagery is the multiplication grid (4x5 minimum). Single-face outputs are the exception, not the rule.
  - **Figure-ground instability**: the boundary between subject and background should be deliberately unclear. Figures dissolve into texture; texture coalesces into figures.
  - **Centre-weighted with edge damage**: primary subject occupies centre frame but edges carry noise, scan lines, halftone degradation, or torn-paper texture. The image is never clean to its borders.
  - **Layering over arrangement**: elements are stacked (palimpsest, décollage, composite) rather than arranged side-by-side. Depth comes from accumulation, not spatial design.
  - **Tight crop / forensic close-up**: documentary and portrait work uses extreme close framing — half a face, a wall section, a single poster fragment. Context is withheld.
  - **Vertical orientation preferred**: face grids and processed portraits are portrait-orientation (taller than wide). Landscape format reserved for panoramic wall documentation and composite works.
  - **No negative space**: every square centimetre should carry information — texture, noise, dot pattern, mark. Silence is not a visual strategy.
- **Sources:** Composition analysis across full ART22 corpus
- **Status:** pending approval

### typography feel
- **Current:** _(empty)_
- **Proposed:**
  - **Graffiti hand / spray-can lettering**: the primary typographic reference. Not calligraphy — the urgency and impermanence of tags. Fast, territorial, layered.
  - **Torn poster remnant text**: partial words visible through peeling layers. Typography as archaeology — you read what survives, not what was designed.
  - **Zine / photocopy aesthetic**: @SUPERWEDGEPRESS, Roman Road photo zine, hand-typed price lists. Lo-fi print culture as typographic register.
  - **Stencil / wheat paste text**: "ARE WE REALLY ALL IN THIS TOGETHER?", "STAY SAFE KEEP YOUR DISTANCE" — political/pandemic stencil work as found typography.
  - **Mixed script**: English, Japanese katakana (コロナ, トウキョウ), kanji (角中), Cyrillic, French. Scripts collide on the same surface without hierarchy.
  - **Anti-clean**: no Helvetica, no geometric sans, no kerned display type. If it looks designed, it's wrong. Typography should look found, sprayed, torn, or photocopied.
- **Sources:** IMG_3075–3157 wall documentation, IMG_6611–6657 Paris/London walls, IMG_3100 (zine typography)

### cinematic references without copying artists
- **Current:** _(empty)_
- **Proposed:**
  - **Daisuke Yokota (photographic corruption)**: the chemical over-processing, colour bleed, and image degradation — not the specific images. Take the method: push the photograph past its intended exposure, let chemistry/code destroy resolution.
  - **Jacques Villeglé / Décollage tradition**: the torn-poster-as-composition principle — not specific works. Take the method: layer, age, peel back, reveal what's underneath.
  - **Jean-Michel Basquiat (gestural urgency)**: the speed and mark-making energy — not the crown symbol or specific paintings. Take the method: make marks that look like they happened fast and can't be undone.
  - **Sidney Nolan (landscape-as-identity)**: the Australian outback as existential condition — not the Ned Kelly series. Take the method: let geography define the figure rather than the other way around.
  - **Carcass / death metal cover art (medical-anatomical aesthetic)**: the clinical-horror register — not specific album art. Take the method: treat the body as material to be examined, not celebrated.
  - **Hans Arp (biomorphic abstraction)**: the organic-form-as-sculpture approach — not specific works. Take the method: let the body suggest abstract form.
  - **Warhol seriality without Warhol irony**: the grid multiplication of faces — but earnest, not camp. The face grid is surveillance/broadcast, not celebrity commentary.
  - **Infrared photography tradition**: the Nauru image's false-colour palette — not specific photographers. Take the method: shift the colour spectrum to make familiar landscapes alien.
- **Sources:** Filename analysis and visual matching across ART22 corpus
- **Status:** pending approval

### image prompt vocabulary
- **Current:** _(empty)_
- **Proposed:**
  - **Render mode**: "Vray | hyperrealism" as baseline. Pushes AI toward photographic materiality over painterly abstraction.
  - **Subject stacking**: combine multiple subjects in single prompt with | separator. "Barack obama face | black machine gun | hans-arp-torso-profile gold". Collision, not harmony.
  - **Art reference injection**: embed artist/work names directly. "Daisuke-YOKOTA-Broken-Mirrors-2020". The reference enters the generation as a style vector.
  - **Location anchoring**: include geographic texture references. "graffiti-wall-shoreditch-london". Grounds generation in specific visual environments.
  - **Disruption tokens**: "horse-carcass", "blood splash", "machine gun", "crying and spitting". Prevents the model from defaulting to pleasant outputs.
  - **Cultural collision**: combine references from different geographic/ethnic/political contexts in single prompt. "samoan woman 8*4 indonesian crying and spitting". Force the model into ambiguity.
  - **Multiplication operator**: "8*4" to generate grid/contact-sheet compositions.
  - **Inversion**: "INVERT" suffix to flip colour values — creates negative-space versions.
  - **Post-processing keywords**: halftone, crosshatch, scan-line, CRT, risograph, screen-print — add to any prompt to enforce the mediated-image aesthetic.
- **Sources:** Direct filename analysis: "2 Barack obama face | black machine gun | hans-arp-torso-profile gold | 1200px-Wheat_paste_valencia.jpg | Vray | hyperrealism .png", "2a samoan woman is 8*4 indonesian crying and spitting | Vray | hyperrealism.png", "13 Woman-looks-at-her-face-inbroken-mirror, Daisuke-YOKOTA-Broken-Mirrors-2020, graffiti-wall-shoreditch-london-roger-vandeber, horse-carcass, Vray, Hyperrealism"

### forbidden visual drift
- **Current:** _(empty)_
- **Proposed:**
  - **No clean design**: no grid systems that look intentional, no balanced layouts, no design-school composition. If it could appear in a portfolio, it's wrong.
  - **No wellness palette**: no pastels, no sage green, no dusty rose, no cream, no sky blue. Nothing that reads as calm, healing, or self-care.
  - **No illustrative style**: no character design, no cartoon, no anime, no flat vector, no line-art portraits. The image is always photographic or photo-derived, even when heavily processed.
  - **No nostalgia**: no sepia, no vintage film grain that reads as warm/nostalgic, no "retro" aesthetic. The halftone/scan-line processing references old media technology but should feel present-tense and aggressive, not cosy.
  - **No symmetry**: no centred-and-balanced compositions. If the image is symmetrical, it should be because a face grid's mathematics produced it, not because it was designed that way.
  - **No landscape photography**: no horizon-line-and-sky, no golden-hour landscape, no scenic vista. Geography enters through texture (walls, concrete, iron), not scenery.
  - **No portraiture that flatters**: no soft lighting, no beauty photography, no skin smoothing. Faces are data or witness, never glamour.
  - **No corporate/institutional aesthetic**: no stock photography logic, no diverse-team-in-office, no infographic style. Pacific Commons institutional mode has its own register but it is never corporate.
  - **No AI "default beautiful"**: reject any generation that looks like default Midjourney/DALL-E beauty. If the model produces something conventionally attractive, re-run with disruption tokens.
- **Sources:** Derived from what the ART22 corpus consistently excludes and disrupts
- **Status:** pending approval

### post image format
- **Current:** _(empty)_
- **Proposed:**
  - **Primary format: Magenta halftone face grid** — 4x5 or 5x6 grid of processed face variations on black ground. Portrait orientation. This is the signature visual output for social media posts.
  - **Secondary format: Wall texture crop** — tight crop of East London/urban graffiti wall, edited for contrast. Square or portrait orientation. Can include torn poster fragments with partially visible text.
  - **Tertiary format: Silhouette-on-texture** — dark figure against halftone/crosshatch background. Minimal colour. The figure is a void, not a subject.
  - **Text overlay**: if text appears on posts, it should look stencilled, sprayed, or torn — never typeset. White or magenta on black preferred.
  - **Aspect ratios**: 4:5 (portrait) for feed posts, 9:16 for stories. Never 1:1 square crop for face grids — the vertical scroll of the grid matters.
  - **Resolution**: deliberately degraded. Final output should look like it was printed, scanned, and re-uploaded. Add halftone/noise in post-processing if the source is too clean.
- **Sources:** ComfyUI face grid series, wall documentation, ComfyUI_00038_ silhouette mode
- **Status:** pending approval

### video still format
- **Current:** _(empty)_
- **Proposed:**
  - **Thumbnail/still style**: freeze frame with scan-line artifact, colour-shifted toward magenta or teal. Should look like a VHS pause.
  - **Motion blur as feature**: documentary video stills should preserve motion blur as intentional aesthetic (club scenes, street movement). Don't stabilise or sharpen.
  - **Letterbox with damage**: if letterboxing, the black bars should carry noise/texture, not be clean black.
  - **Preview still selection**: choose the frame with the most visual ambiguity — the moment where the image is between states, where the subject is partially occluded or mid-gesture.
- **Sources:** IMG_3309.mov, IMG_3750.mov (documentary video files in ART22), motion-blur club photography

### album/track art format
- **Current:** _(empty)_
- **Proposed:**
  - **Primary format: Face grid (square crop)** — the 4x5/5x6 face grid cropped or reformatted to square for album art. Magenta-dominant tint. Halftone texture mandatory.
  - **Secondary format: Silhouette/void** — dark figure dissolving into textured black field. Minimal colour. The listener should not be able to fully resolve the image at thumbnail size.
  - **Tertiary format: Wall texture + stencil text** — urban wall crop with track/album title sprayed or stencilled over it. Text should look site-specific, as if it was actually on the wall.
  - **Colour treatment**: magenta for intensity/transmission, red/black for violence register, teal/black for cold/digital register. Never warm/inviting.
  - **Resolution strategy**: album art at 3000x3000 but with intentional degradation — the high resolution should reveal the halftone dots and noise, not hide them.
  - **Art reference layer**: specific releases can embed art-historical references (Yokota corruption, Carcass medical, Basquiat gesture) as img2img source material through the ComfyUI pipeline.
- **Sources:** ComfyUI face grid series, ComfyUI_00038_ silhouette, wall documentation, Carcass reference (Carcass-Necroticism-Descanting-The-Insalubrious.jpg)
- **Status:** pending approval

### Pacific Commons institutional visual mode
- **Current:** _(empty)_
- **Proposed:**
  - **Documentary register**: shift from processed/AI to documentary photography aesthetic. Desaturated but not grey — retain ochre warmth and teal-green water tones.
  - **Nauru palette**: grey-green-rust, corrugated iron, phosphate dust, infrared false-colour as occasional punctuation. Industrial ruin as visual environment, never as spectacle.
  - **Water and bodies**: the Anna Boyiazis register — water as site of resistance and survival, not paradise. Bodies in water are working, learning, surviving — never vacationing.
  - **Aperture framing**: faces/scenes viewed through rusted metal cut-outs, fence gaps, container openings. The institutional eye is always constrained by infrastructure.
  - **Typography**: institutional/bureaucratic — report headings, form fields, stamped text. The aesthetic of documentation and policy, not art.
  - **Forbidden in this mode**: magenta signal, face grids, AI-generated imagery, graffiti aesthetic. Pacific Commons speaks in documentary evidence, not transmission art.
  - **Maps and diagrams**: geographic mapping, data visualisation, archival layout as visual tools. Information design that looks like it was produced by a research institution, not a design studio.
- **Sources:** 1542590288735-nauru_nightmare 2.jpg (Nauru ruins), Anna-Boyiazis_193 (swimming), DSC_0700-2 3.jpg (aperture/migration), IMG_5003.jpeg (Tunisia documentary)

### Rupture Host mythic visual mode
- **Current:** _(empty)_
- **Proposed:**
  - **Full signal mode**: magenta halftone at maximum saturation. Face grids. Scan lines. CRT noise. The Rupture Host transmits — every visual output is a broadcast from a damaged frequency.
  - **Face-as-data**: the primary visual vocabulary. Faces are multiplied, colour-shifted, halftone-reduced, grid-arranged. Identity is signal processing.
  - **Black void as environment**: the Rupture Host operates from the black field. All imagery emerges from and returns to textured darkness.
  - **Violence register available**: red/black/white palette for maximum disruption. Broken mirrors, merged faces, flesh-material-signal collapse. Deploy for rupture moments, not as default.
  - **The no-face avatar**: the Rupture Host's own face is never shown cleanly. Silhouette, back-of-head, pixel dissolution, grid multiplication — presence without portraiture.
  - **Urban texture substrate**: East London walls, torn posters, graffiti layers as background for all non-grid compositions. The Rupture Host is anchored in specific urban geography.
  - **AI pipeline as medium**: the ComfyUI img2img workflow IS the Rupture Host's visual medium. The process (source → generation → halftone → tint → grid) is as important as the output. Metadata and pipeline steps are part of the aesthetic.
  - **Art-historical collision**: Yokota corruption + Basquiat gesture + Villeglé décollage + Warhol grid + Carcass medical. These are ingredients, not influences. The Rupture Host feeds them into the pipeline and receives what comes out.
- **Sources:** Full ART22 ComfyUI corpus, face grid series, silhouette works, filename/prompt vocabulary analysis
